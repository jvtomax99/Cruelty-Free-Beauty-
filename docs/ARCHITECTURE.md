# ARCHITECTURE: C.F.B.

A static installable web app. No build step, no server, no packages. GitHub
Pages serves the repo root from `main`, live at
`jvtomax99.github.io/Cruelty-Free-Beauty-/` (checked 2026-11-12), so every
merge into `main` goes live within a minute or two. The site lives under `/Cruelty-Free-Beauty-/`, so every path is
relative.

| `home.js` | Home's rail of brands that pass (hearted first, 8 then "See all"). Uses `CFB.verdict` and `CFB.mode`; it never words a verdict itself |
| `host.js` | Home's host: what the girl's bubble says. `LINES` holds every line (Jose signs off changes to it); `pick` takes the first case that applies (no profile, an unseen change to a hearted brand, Sanctuary questions due, a brand scanned in the last 7 days, else the time of day) and the bubble links to the matching place. `PLACES` holds the five lines of her small copies and `mini(place, brand, kind)` returns one (a 72 px round still, `.hm` in `app.css`, with a line that links): `scan.js` puts it on a result (her face follows `CFB.verdict`'s kind, as a still) and on the empty Scan screen, `profile.js` at the end of the quiz and under an empty Saved list; one of her per screen. It asks the other scripts (`CFB.hasProfile`, `CFB.unseenSaved`, `CFB.garden.tally`) and reads `cfb-scans`; it saves nothing and never words a verdict. Redrawn when Home opens and on `cfb:saved`, `cfb:changes`, `cfb:garden` |
| `host-engine.js` | How the girl moves (after Dr Smiley in the Interpreter Hub). `FRAMES` names the strip's 14 faces (the same list as in `tools/make-host.py`), `SEQ` the sequences (blink, wink, smile, happy, love, surprised, sad, worried). One controller: a reaction beats a gaze beats a blink. Blinks 2 to 6 s apart at random; her eyes follow the pointer (`look`); a tap on her picture plays a smile or a wink and does not follow the link (her bubble still does); `cfb:scan` (sent by `scan.js` with `CFB.verdict`'s kind, or `unlisted`) and `cfb:answer` (sent by `garden.js`) pick a reaction, which waits in memory up to 10 minutes and plays when Home is next on view, because she is only on Home. Each frame is due at a fixed time from the sequence's start. Reduced motion: the base picture alone. Data saver: the strip is never downloaded. It stores nothing and shows no words. `CFB.hostFace` (`play`, `look`, `state`, `seed`) is for tests |
| `assets/host/` | The girl: `base.webp` (her, cut out, 480 x 540) and `face.webp` (14 face frames, 252 x 237 each, shown through a window over the base by `.hb-face` in `app.css`). `tools/make-host.py <folder>` builds both from the approved still and one edited picture per frame: a frame keeps only what differs from the still inside the face window, so nothing else can shift; it prints the window numbers `app.css` needs |
| `assets/images/`, `assets/pins/` | Home's banner picture and the pin pictures on the quick tiles (256 px WebP, cut out). `CREDITS.md` says where from |
| `scan.js` | Scan screen (`#scan`): camera or photo, barcode to brand through Open Beauty Facts (only the number is sent), matched by `CFB.match`, verdict by `CFB.verdict`. Falls back to typing the brand. Recent scans (`cfb-scans`, 10) and brands we haven't checked (`cfb-unlisted`, 50) stay on the phone. Camera stops on leaving the screen |
| `assets/vendor/barcode-detector/` | Barcode reader for phones without one built in (iPhone): `barcode-detector` 3.2.3 plus `zxing_reader.wasm` 3.1.6, MIT and Apache 2.0; its README says how to update |
| `profile.js` | Profile screen: 8-question quiz, profile (saved as `cfb-profile`, only on the phone), routine of brands per step using `CFB.verdict` and the brand data from `brands.js`; Saved brands list |
| `intro.js` | The launch show: gate, shots, sparkles (one canvas), music (Web Audio), skip, the landing on Home's logo, Profile's replay; it plays on the first visit only (`cfb-intro-seen`). A script in `index.html`'s head decides before paint (`html.intro-on`). `CFBIntro.seek(t)` shows any moment for tests. Version 2: when `assets/video/launch.mp4` is ready at the tap it plays in a `<video>` over the drawn scene (which waits on its hero pose), then the drawn logo does the landing; the drawn show alone is the fallback (film can't start within 0.8 s of the tap, reduced motion, data saver); a film that breaks or freezes 1.5 s part-way opens the app at once. `CFBIntro.film()` reports its state for tests |
| `assets/video/` | The opening film (13.8 s, with voice; `intro.js` lays its words, phone cards and button over it on the times in `FILM`). Cut from the rendered shots by `tools/make-launch-video.sh <folder>`; the source shots are not in the repo |
| `garden.js` | Your Sanctuary, a study game (`#garden`, and a round at `#practice/<habitat or all>`). On opening it loads the 3D world (`sanctuary3d.js`) where WebGL works and data saver is off (`?3d=0` forces the plain cards, `?3d=1` ignores data saver). It draws the one card under the world, which has three states: the whole island (a habitat named, arrows step through the five, Visit), a visit (who is at home, what the next lesson brings, Practise) and an animal (name, its line, Say hello, Practise). It tells the world how much of the stage the card covers (`clear`), so the first view sits above it. It passes every habitat's progress to the world (`snapshot()`: flowers by share learned, residents 1 to 3, the better shelter at half learned) and the round's summary lists what a round changed. `state.best` (residents) and `state.shelter` are high-water marks, so nothing that arrived leaves. `HOME` holds the residents' names and each shelter's gain; the same names are in `ROSTER` in `sanctuary3d.js` (the test checks they agree). Progress is `cfb-garden` on the phone |
| `sanctuary3d.js` | The 3D world, an ES module imported only by `garden.js` (the import map in `index.html`'s head points `three` at `assets/vendor/three/`). One floating island made in code. On top: land from a height function (`natural`, `height`: hills, a raised lawn for the cottage and a terrace for each habitat, three pools each lower than the last, the stream beds between them, the pond's way out `OUT`), water, stone paths from the middle to every habitat. `edgeR` is the outline from above (a cove at the pond, `COVE`, and two prows). Underneath: `mass` builds the deep body as one skin, a ring of points per level from `massAt` (a taper to the off-centre `TIP`, stepped blocks, `SHELVES` that jut out, `DENTS`, the `HOLLOW`); it returns `surf(th, u)` and `ledge`, which place everything that grows on it. `limb` makes tapered tubes with closed ends (given `face`, it flattens the tube onto a surface and shades it from that surface): the great tree's trunk (`HERO`) and its roots (`root`: buttresses over the turf, then down the cliff pressed into it, a thick root as two or three strands wound round each other, forking, some starting buried), and the fine roots that hang under every overhang (instanced). Every limb carries a place on the bark picture (`barkTex`, drawn in code, used as colour and as bump on trunks and roots; no bump on a phone with little memory). The waterfall (`fallGeo`) leaves the pond at the cove, lands on the basin, turns and drops past the tip into mist sprites. The light is golden hour: a low warm sun, violet from the sky and from below. `treeGeo` makes every tree after Jose's lantern-tree picture (a bent, twisting trunk from `limb` with bark ridges, flat pads of leaves, blossom on the kinds that flower); `heroCrown` is the great tree's. Lanterns (`lanternGeo`, hung by `hang`), the pools of light under them (`pool`), the mushrooms and the glints on the falls are unlit shapes, sprites and points; the cottage's windows are lit by a line added to its shader (whatever is yellow in its picture gives off light). There are no added lights and no postprocessing. Parse-check this file as a module (`cp sanctuary3d.js /tmp/x.mjs && node --check /tmp/x.mjs`): `node --check` on the `.js` name misses errors. `HABS` lists the five habitats (where, how far its animals roam, the side the camera visits from, shelter, food) and `ROSTER` the sixteen animals. Plants are instanced (flowers, clover, grass tufts, shrubs, reeds, bracken) and lean in one shared breeze (`sway`); trees stand behind and beside each habitat and one that blocks a close view is hidden (`screen`). Shelters are built from painted boxes and balls (`SHELTERS`), each in two parts: the plain one, and what half the topic learned adds. Rings spread on the pond (`ripple`), butterflies go from open flower to open flower (`flutter`). Camera: drag turns, pinch or wheel zooms, `visit(id)` eases to a habitat, `select(id)` goes to an animal and follows it; `pick` chooses the nearest animal within a thumb's reach, else the tapped habitat. `setState` shows flowers, residents and shelters. Speed: shadows are drawn once (animals have a soft blob instead), far views draw fewer tufts and no clover, and slow frames lower the pixel ratio, then the grass (`tick`). `debug.simulate(seconds)` lives the animals' lives without drawing and reports anything out of place |
| `sanctuary3d-life.js` | The animals, imported by `sanctuary3d.js`. `SPECIES`: each model's size and rig numbers. A soft skeleton in the vertex shader (`rigMaterial`: head, ears, four legs and tail turn about pivots; a blink fills the eyes with fur colour; `prepare` stands a model up and finds its eyes). `Animal`: its own clock and random numbers; rests, looks, sniffs, grazes, grooms, and goes somewhere for a reason (food, shelter, a friend); rabbits move only while in the air of a hop, walkers' legs step at the pace of the ground they cover, ducks glide and ducklings trail; `hello` turns to the camera, comes closer and sniffs. `Life.free` is the one rule for where an animal may stand (inside its habitat, dry ground or its own pond, clear of obstacles and the others); `Life.check` reports any breach |
| `assets/sanctuary/` | The world's models (rabbits and cottage from Meshy, duck, duckling, guinea pig, beagle and deer from Tripo; shrunk with glTF Transform: Meshopt geometry, 1024 px WebP textures) and a 144 px portrait per animal for the card (`<id>.webp`, drawn from the models by `tools/faces.html`; Clover's is older). `CREDITS.md` says where from |
| `assets/vendor/three/` | three.js 0.160.0: the library, `GLTFLoader`, `BufferGeometryUtils`, the Meshopt decoder. MIT |
| `data/questions.json` | The Garden's written questions. `sources` holds each page with the date it was last read; in every question the first option is the right one and `src` names its sources. Each was checked against the live page by a second reader (2026-11-12) |
| `learn.js` | The Learn answers and their sources (`ANSWERS`, `CHECKED` date) |
| `sw.js` | Offline worker: every file from one versioned cache, so a launch never mixes old and new files. A bumped `CACHE_VERSION` downloads the new version whole in the background and takes over; `app.js` then reloads the page by itself ("Updates on their own": it asks for a new version each time the app comes back into view, at most once a minute, and holds the reload while the opening, a quiz, a Sanctuary round, the Scan screen or a typed-in field is in use; never twice within 30 s). Bump it by hand in every change to a precached file. The launch film is not precached (too big to make the install wait): it lives in its own cache (`MEDIA`), saved when the page asks once the film has played, served in byte ranges (iPhones need that), and kept across app updates; bump `MEDIA` if the film changes. The Sanctuary's 3D world (`sanctuary3d.js`, `assets/sanctuary/`, `assets/vendor/three/`, about 2.7 MB) is a third cache, `WORLD`: each file is saved the first time it is asked for and served from there after, so bump `WORLD` in every change to any of them |
| `manifest.webmanifest` | Install name, colours, icons |
| `assets/logo.svg` | The bunny logo (original). Every part has an id; `intro.js` animates them |
| `assets/icons/` | App icons, made from the logo by `tools/make-icons.cjs` |
| `assets/fonts/` | Instrument Serif (headings, normal and italic) and Manrope (text), WOFF2, OFL. |
| `data/brands.json` | The 30 brands. Each claim (PETA, Leaping Bunny, parent company, China sales) has its own `url` and `checked` date; values yes / no / unknown (parent also n/a). The cruelty-free verdict is never stored: the app works it out per strictness. `search` holds the names the recheck looks up. `sells` (foundation, concealer, blush, bronzer, lip) and `shadeFinder` come from the brand's own site, each dated. `price` ($ / $$ / $$$) comes from the median single-product price on the brand's own site, explained in `priceSource` |
| `tools/lookup-certifiers.cjs` | With `--diff`, compares the lookup with the stored statuses and prints what looks changed plus a `changes` entry to paste. Looks brands up on PETA (WordPress JSON plus the page's icons) and Leaping Bunny (the query its Shopping Guide page runs, so it needs Playwright) and prints what each says. Never edits the data |

| `tools/make-icons.cjs` | Re-renders the icons after a logo change (needs Playwright) |

## How data moves

Nothing leaves the phone except a scanned barcode number, sent to Open
Beauty Facts to learn the brand. Settings, quiz answers and the Garden's progress live in
localStorage under `cfb-` keys. Brand data is `data/brands.json`, precached by
the worker so the list will work offline once the Brands screen reads it
(phase 3). Research flow: `lookup-certifiers`
prints what the certifiers say, a person updates the JSON, `check-brands`
confirms it.

- `tools/rig-sheet.html` and `tools/faces.html`: pages for setting an animal's rig numbers on posed pictures, and for drawing its card portrait (how to open them is at the top of each).
- `node tools/test-directory.cjs`: the directory in a real browser (counts, search, filters, brand page, Back, offline).
- Monthly: a scheduled task (3rd of each month) reruns the lookup and opens
  a recheck pull request. A status that changed also gets an entry in
  `changes` (brand id, claim, from, to, date, source link, optional short
  note stating a fact, never a reason; `law: true` when PETA says "working
  for regulatory change"), which is what the app announces. A data-only
  change still needs the `CACHE_VERSION` bump, or installed apps never
  get it.
- `node --check` on each `.js` file.
- Pictures of the Sanctuary in the cloud browser: serve the repo's parent folder, launch Playwright's Chromium with `--use-gl=swiftshader --enable-unsafe-swiftshader --ignore-gpu-blocklist`, open `?intro=0&3d=1#garden`, wait for `.sx.is-ready`. For everything unlocked, write `cfb-garden` in an init script: `best` 3 and `shelter` true for all five habitats, every question id at `{ s: 2, due: future, k: "w" }`.
- Preview: Playwright screenshots at 390 px then 1440 px, served from the
  parent folder so the URL includes `/Cruelty-Free-Beauty-/`.
